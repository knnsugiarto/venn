# Your Circle add-on

Follow the uploaded brief (saved as `docs/ADDON-your-circle.md`); it wins over SPEC and the theme-packs add-on. Work happens in this draft only.

## What people will see
- Answers build up a saved **Your Circle** on the phone. The QR code is always made fresh from it.
- Home has three states: **new** (intro + "Start my circle (1 min)" + "Or start with a theme"), **almost ready** (circle + "N more answers and your code is ready." + "Finish in 30 seconds"), **ready** (circle, name, "3 of 8 themes", QR on the first screen, theme grid, "Clear my circle").
- Each theme = its 5 taps + 1–2 "In your own words" prompts, ending on a finish screen.
- Scanning: ready goes straight to the Venn, almost-ready answers only what's missing, new users get the 5-question quick start.
